# Office interaction audio

- `iphone-ping.mp3`: user-supplied phone notification recording, retained unchanged. Playback skips the first 1.08 seconds of silence so the ping starts with the tap.
- `office-growl.mp3`: “Male Angry Growl.wav” by usamah, [Freesound 464993](https://freesound.org/people/usamah/sounds/464993/), CC0. Trimmed to a 1.5-second reaction with short fades.
- `office-slurp.mp3`: “Drink Sip and Swallow” by OwlStorm / Ashe Kirk, [Freesound 320139](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/320139/), CC0. Short fades and mono conversion.
- `office-fan.mp3`: user-supplied `vacuum.mp3`, trimmed to 5.2 seconds with a 0.6-second fade in and 0.7-second fade out. Playback gain is 0.035, keeping it beneath the typing.
- `office-success.mp3`: original synthesized two-note glass chime (G5, C6), generated locally.
- `office-lamp.mp3`: user-supplied `lamp.mp3`; the first switch action (1.01–1.43 seconds), with short edge fades so it responds immediately to both on and off taps.
- `deep-house.mp3`: user-supplied track from Downloads, copied unchanged. Loops through a separate streaming audio element at 30% volume; the Sonos toggles playback and the page sound control mutes it without resetting its position.

Freesound licenses checked on 2026-10-13. All effects are served locally and controlled by the page's speaker button.
